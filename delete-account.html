Delete your account

Not Fussed by hello@getnotfussed.com

In the app (quickest)

Open Not Fussed, tap the settings button on the home screen, then tap Delete my account and confirm. Your account and email address are deleted straight away.

By email

Email hello@getnotfussed.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

Your account and the email address linked to it. We don't keep your photos. Your saved recipes, ratings and settings are only on your phone: deleting the app removes them. Anonymous usage counts (how many times the app was used each day) aren't linked to you and are kept.
