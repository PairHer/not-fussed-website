Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

Who we are

Not Fussed is run from Jersey and follows the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018. Contact: hello@getnotfussed.com.

What stays on your phone

Your diet settings, dislikes, cupboard basics, ratings and saved recipes are kept on your phone. They're sent to our server only as part of a request to pick a dish, and aren't stored there.

Photos and videos

Fridge photos and cooking photos are sent securely to our server and on to our AI provider (Anthropic) to read them, then discarded. For a cooking video, only a few still frames are sent: never the video or its sound. We don't keep your photos.

Your account

If you sign in, we store your email address so you can sign in again. Signing in is optional. We'll never sell your email or send you marketing without asking.

Usage and offers

The app creates a random ID for your phone. We use it to apply the daily limit, and to count how many times partner offers are shown or used. Partners only ever see totals, never who you are.

Who processes data for us

Supabase (hosting, database and sign-in) and Anthropic (the AI). They only process data to provide their service to us.

Your rights

To delete your account, go to Settings in the app and tap "Delete my account", or email hello@getnotfussed.com from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it within 30 days. You can also ask to see or correct your data at hello@getnotfussed.com. If you're unhappy with how we handle it, you can complain to the Jersey Office of the Information Commissioner.

Age

Not Fussed is for people aged 16 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
